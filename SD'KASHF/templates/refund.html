Refund Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Deposit & Discovery Phase

Initial project deposits reserve resources and kickstart strategy/wireframing phases. If a project is canceled prior to wireframe sign-off or design mockups, up to 80% of the deposit may be refunded depending on labor completed.

2. Development & Milestone Payments

Once wireframes/designs are approved and active technical development begins, payments for completed milestone stages are non-refundable as they cover engineer and designer labor hours.

3. Project Cancellation

If a client cancels a project mid-way through production, the client will only be billed for the hours and work completed up to the date of formal cancellation notice.

4. Satisfaction Guarantee & Revisions

We build dedicated revision loops into all plans to ensure deliverables match agreed specs. Refunds are not issued for design preference changes after final project sign-off.

5. How to Request a Refund

To submit a request, contact your project manager directly or reach out through the WebMek account portal with your project ID and reason for review.
