Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you directly provide when filling out forms, submitting project inquiries, registering an account, or communicating with us. This includes contact details, company names, project requirements, and uploaded documents.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected to deliver requested services, process project milestones, manage client accounts, send project updates, improve platform performance, and respond to support queries.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell or rent personal information to third parties. We only share necessary operational data with trusted technical partners (such as database hosting or payment processors) under strict confidentiality agreements.

4. Security

WebMek uses industry-standard encryption protocols (SSL/TLS), secure access controls, and hashed authentication mechanisms to protect client data from loss, unauthorized access, or disclosure.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to your personal data, request corrections, or request complete account deletion at any time by contacting our privacy team.
